FAQ

Common Questions

How much does a consultation cost?

Your initial consultation is completely free. We don't charge until we close a deal together.

How quickly can you list my home?

Typically within 5–7 business days of signing. We handle professional photography, staging advice, and MLS listing.

Do you offer free home valuations?

Yes. We provide a Comparative Market Analysis (CMA) at no charge to give you an accurate picture of your home's value.

Do you work with buyers relocating to Austin?

Absolutely. We specialize in relocation services — virtual tours, neighborhood matching, school district analysis, and concierge onboarding.
